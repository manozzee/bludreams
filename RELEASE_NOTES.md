## BluDreams v1.1.0

Welcome to the initial public release of **BluDreams**!

### ✨ Highlights
- **Minimalist Pinterest-Inspired UI**: Clean, distraction-free media browsing styled with **Outfit** and **Figtree** typography.
- **Smooth Streaming Experience**: Responsive playback controls and fluid media loading designed for uninterrupted viewing.
- **Improved Playback Stability**: Enhanced player reliability across streams, subtitles, and background playback transitions.

### 🛠️ Improvements & Fixes
- **Navigation Transitions**: Smoother screen transitions and more responsive tab/fragment switching.
- **Smaller Screen Layouts**: Refined spacing, padding, and card sizing on compact phone displays.
- **Performance Optimizations**: Reduced UI overhead and faster startup/scrolling across feeds and search results.
